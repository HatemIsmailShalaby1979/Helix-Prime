Why this exists

Built from 28 years on the operations floor.

Hatem Shalaby spent nearly three decades running operations. He saw the same problems everywhere:

  • Schedules that never match reality.
  • Problems discovered days after they start.
  • Good people leaving before anyone notices.
  • Decisions made from gut feeling, not data.

So he built an AI team that fixes those problems — and, just as importantly, one that never takes control away from people.

How it stays honest

Your team stays in charge. Always.

This isn't a promise in a brochure — these checks are built into the software and verified by tests. The AI proposes. A person decides.

Nothing important happens without approval

Every significant action is paused and handed to a human first. The system suggests; you decide.

No one approves their own work

The role that suggests a change can never be the one that approves it. No shortcuts, no self-dealing.

Every decision is recorded

A permanent, tamper-proof record shows what was decided, why, and the data behind it. You can always check the history.

A safety switch

If anything ever looks wrong, one switch pauses the whole system instantly. Nothing risky happens until you say it's OK.

Your data stays yours

Each client's information is kept separate and private. One part of the system can never see another's data.

Safe, honest testing first

Right now the system runs on realistic demo data, not live customer data. Real connections and live writes are switched off until the pilot is approved.
